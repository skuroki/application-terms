# White Noise Player Support

Thank you for using White Noise Player.

## How to Use the App

White Noise Player starts playing white noise when you open the app. Use your device's volume controls to adjust the sound level. Close the app or stop audio playback on your device when you no longer want to listen.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I cannot hear any sound

Make sure your device's volume is turned up and that audio is not being sent to connected headphones, speakers, or another AirPlay device.

### The sound is coming from another device

Check your device's audio output settings and disconnect any Bluetooth or AirPlay device you do not want to use.

### The sound stops unexpectedly

Another app, a phone call, or a system audio event may interrupt playback. Open White Noise Player again to resume the sound.
